# One "[section]" is one boot: a crash loses only the rest of its section.
# A step is "name route [route ...]"; the routes go 0.7 s apart, the shot is
# taken WALK_SHOT seconds after the first. tab/N pops that tab to its root:
# 0 Contacts, 1 Chats, 2 Settings on the bench account (no Calls tab).
# Public chats stand in for the account's own: telegram (channel), tdlibchat
# (supergroup). Nothing here sends, joins or changes a setting.
