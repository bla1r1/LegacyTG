- The stamp is laid out once, by `TGMessageStampPlace` (eye, count, time, ticks, right to left), and the cells place the frames it returns. The channel count no longer drifts from the eye or cuts the time to "2.1M…"; a sent document keeps room for its ticks; a contact's time has its own line above Call and Add.
